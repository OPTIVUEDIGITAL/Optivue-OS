Cookies and browser storage

Cookies are small records stored by a browser and sent to the matching website. Local storage stays in the browser until cleared. Session storage normally lasts for the tab session. Scripts and embedded services can also send data without setting cookies.

Categories

Strictly necessary records remember privacy choices and support the estimate you request. Hosting processes requests to deliver the site. Functional storage remembers your theme, and Calendly provides requested booking. Analytics measures website use only after permission. No marketing pixels or marketing cookie integration are configured in this website. Adding one requires an updated choice.

Your choices

Optional categories default off everywhere. Accept all enables available optional categories. Reject all keeps them off. Customize lets you save Analytics and Functional choices separately. No option starts preselected on a fresh visit. Strictly necessary records remain available for their stated purposes.

We remember your choice for 6 calendar months in a first-party record, using local storage only if the cookie cannot be written. The record contains the consent version, time and category choices, not a personal identifier. A changed consent version or expired record asks again.

reopens preferences. Withdrawal stops our event sending and clears accessible first-party analytics cookies and journey storage. A page reload removes the already-loaded Google library. We cannot delete cookies on a third party’s own domain.

Global Privacy Control

Global Privacy Control causes optional categories to default off, including a saved choice made outside preferences. You may explicitly change your choices in the preference center. Browser privacy settings also let you block or clear cookies and site storage. Consult your browser’s privacy settings. Blocking required storage may prevent your choices or estimate from being remembered.

Calendly

Booking is handled by Calendly, which has its own privacy practices and may set cookies. Before the first embed loads, choose Continue to booking. This enables functional storage for the consent period. Later booking requests open the same Calendly service directly. Refusing analytics does not prevent booking. No Calendly iframe or script loads on initial page view. Calendly controls the cookies and storage inside its booking service. Their names and lifetimes vary with its service and your choices. Review Calendly’s Privacy Notice. This site does not suppress Calendly’s own privacy controls.
