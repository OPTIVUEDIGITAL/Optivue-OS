Retention

Retention is based on the purpose of a record, the status of your request or engagement, legal recordkeeping needs and disputes. Records under our control should be deleted or anonymized when no longer needed for these purposes. Booking records: For arranging and following up on the requested call, and for any resulting engagement or applicable recordkeeping obligation. Correspondence: For handling the request and related business relationship, resolving disputes, and meeting applicable recordkeeping obligations. Analytics: Browser identifiers expire after 180 days without automatic renewal. Data already collected is retained separately under the GA4 property settings and Google policies; clearing cookies does not delete those records. Hosting logs: Provider-managed delivery and security records follow the hosting providers’ retention rules, including any security or legal exceptions. Browser storage durations appear in the Cookie Policy. Clearing browser storage does not delete records held by providers.

Security

The site uses HTTPS and restricts booking-message handling to the expected Calendly origin and frame. Analytics event fields are restricted. These measures do not eliminate all risk. Do not send passwords or sensitive records through this website.

Your rights

Rights depend on the law applicable to you and the operator, and may include exceptions.

UK and EEA

Applicable rights include access, correction, erasure, restriction, portability, objection and withdrawal of consent. You may complain to your supervisory authority, including the UK ICO.

California

Where the CCPA/CPRA applies, rights include knowing, deleting and correcting personal information, opting out of sale or sharing, and non-discrimination. This site treats Global Privacy Control as rejection of optional storage unless you expressly override it in preferences. This website has no advertising-audience or sale/sharing opt-out workflow because it does not implement those activities. This statement concerns the website described in this policy.

Canada

Where PIPEDA or other applicable law provides these rights, you may request access, challenge accuracy and withdraw consent, subject to lawful limits. Complaints may be made to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

Australia

Where the Privacy Act and Australian Privacy Principles apply, you may request access and correction and complain to the OAIC.

Philippines

The Data Privacy Act of 2012, RA 10173, provides applicable rights to information, access, objection, rectification, erasure or blocking, portability and remedies. Complaints may be made to the National Privacy Commission.

Send requests to rahmel@optivuedigital.com · +63 997 108 9661. Response timeframe: Within the period required by applicable law, after any proportionate identity verification. We will explain any permitted extension or refusal. Use Cookie settings to withdraw optional browser consent immediately.

Children

This website is intended for adult business users, not children. Do not submit a child’s information. If you believe a child has submitted information, contact the privacy contact. We will review the report and remove information under our control when required by law.

Sensitive and health information

Do not submit patient information, protected health information or personal health data through this website, estimator or booking questions. Any work involving patient information requires a separate review of the scope, agreements, vendors, access and safeguards before that information is introduced. Business registration does not constitute HIPAA certification.

External links and booking

Booking is handled by Calendly, which has its own privacy practices and may set cookies. Before the first embed loads, choose Continue to booking. This enables functional storage for the consent period. Later booking requests open the same Calendly service directly. Refusing analytics does not prevent booking. Third-party websites have their own policies. Our controls do not remove third-party cookies from domains we do not control.
