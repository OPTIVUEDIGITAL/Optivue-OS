Before we work together

Clear expectations from day one.

Who pays for the software?

You purchase and maintain your platform subscriptions directly. Optivue fees cover strategy, architecture, implementation, and the ongoing services you select. Usage charges are separate.

What happens if I end the retainer?

You keep the agreed delivered workflows, configurations, and documentation under the contract. Support ends after the agreed notice period, and Optivue access is removed as part of handover. Your platform subscriptions remain your responsibility.

How do maintenance and growth work differ?

Maintenance addresses the agreed existing system. Growth work uses analytics and strategy to prioritize changes to the funnel. The Partnership includes both within a defined monthly scope.

Is human support available around the clock?

Support follows the hours and response targets in your agreement. Automated services running outside business hours do not include round-the-clock human support.

What happens before a clinic launches?

Build and test with fictional data first. Before live patient information enters the system, review applicable agreements, safeguards, platform features, and integrations. A platform upgrade alone does not establish HIPAA readiness.
